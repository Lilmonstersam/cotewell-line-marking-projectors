      Not sure a projector is the answer?

      Lean line tape and
      paint solutions.

      A projector is the right call where the floor itself is the problem. Where it is not, a laid or painted line is usually cheaper and faster. We supply and install both, so we will tell you which one your site actually needs.

      Lean line tape

      Peel and stick, down the same day.

      Rolls from 0.5mm to 1.3mm, matched to the traffic that crosses the line. Installs in hours with no curing time and no production downtime, and it lifts cleanly when the layout changes. The practical choice on sound internal concrete.

      • No downtime, nothing to cure
      • Reconfigurable as the site evolves
      • Best on clean, sound internal floors

      Paint and Cold Applied Plastic

      Bonded lines for the hardest floors.

      Two-pack epoxy line marking and Cold Applied Plastic bond into the surface rather than sitting on top of it. This is what we specify for external areas, heavy vehicle turning circles and floors too rough or contaminated for tape.

      • External asphalt and concrete
      • Heavy vehicle and turning traffic
      • Rough, patched or contaminated surfaces
